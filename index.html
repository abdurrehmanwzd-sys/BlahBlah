01 · A LITTLE NOTE FOR YOU
First things first

About Tomorrow

Saanvi Sharma, I know tomorrow might feel a little scary. Maybe you're excited, maybe you're nervous, or maybe you're already thinking about all the things that could go wrong.

But please, meri baat sunna.

Your Marks Are Not Your Worth

Whatever your result says tomorrow, it cannot measure your entire intelligence, your potential, your kindness, or the person you are becoming.

A good result will be something to celebrate. A disappointing result will be something to learn from. Neither one changes your worth.

I'm proud of you for trying, for studying, and for getting through everything that made this exam difficult. You don't have to score a particular percentage to make me proud of you.

And if tomorrow doesn't go the way you hoped, please don't start believing that you're not capable of doing better. One result is one moment in your life, not the definition of your future.

"You are allowed to be disappointed in a result without being disappointed in yourself."

So please, whatever happens, breathe. We'll take things one step at a time. You don't have to pretend to be happy if you're sad, and you don't have to hide your feelings from me.

02 · YOU ARE MORE THAN A RESULT

Please Don't Be Sad

And Saanvi, if your result isn't what you expected, and Papa gets upset or scolds you, please don't think that everything is over.

Papa ka toh kaam hi hai daantna, please tum sad matt hona.

I know being scolded can hurt, especially when you've already been worrying about your result yourself. But a disappointing day doesn't mean that your efforts were meaningless.

Give yourself some time. Let things settle down. You don't need to solve every problem in one day.

04 · MY WORDS, MY FEELINGS
And one last thing

Please Understand Me

And Saanvi, one last thing.

I'm not asking you to become someone else for me. I'm not asking you to change your entire personality or love me exactly the way I love you.

I just want you to understand me. To understand what makes me happy, what hurts me, and what makes me feel loved.

Because I'm Abdurrehman. I'm the same person who tries his best to make you happy, who wants to see you smile even when he isn't feeling okay himself. And sometimes, even I want to feel that someone cares enough to notice when I'm not okay.

I don't want to teach you how to love me. I just want you to know me well enough that, sometimes, you understand without me having to explain everything.

Maybe that's too much to expect sometimes, maybe it isn't. I don't know.

But I know that I don't want to lose you over something we could've talked about. I don't want you to become another person I have to learn to live without.

I want you here, Saanvi. Not because I can't live without you, but because I genuinely want you in my life.

And I hope that, someday, you understand what I mean when I say that I crave the love I give to you.

You don't have to have the perfect words. You don't have to fix everything immediately. I just hope we can understand each other better.
